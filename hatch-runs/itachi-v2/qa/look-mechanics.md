# Itachi look mechanics

Itachi is a humanoid anime shinobi with a rigid planted lower body and a compact cloak silhouette. Direction is expressed through the eyes, eyelids, nose-tip placement, subtle head and neck turn, and restrained upper-body follow-through. Keep the forehead protector, long dark hair, black cloak, red cloud markings, pale face, and red eyes consistent.

- `000` up: face broadly frontal; pupils and nose/face aim toward the top edge.
- `090` screen-right: pupils, nose tip, face plane, and slight head turn lead toward the viewer's right.
- `180` down: face broadly frontal; eyelids, pupils, and nose/face aim toward the bottom edge.
- `270` screen-left: inverse of `090`, with the face plane and gaze clearly leading toward the viewer's left.
- Intermediate directions use even 22.5-degree steps, with eyes leading and head/upper body following subtly.
- Keep feet, lower cloak hem, body height, baseline, and frame scale registered; do not rotate or skew the whole sprite.
- The cloak and hair follow the torso naturally but remain attached; no new props, effects, text, labels, shadows, or guide marks.
